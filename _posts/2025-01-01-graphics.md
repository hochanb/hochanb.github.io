Built raytracing render pipeline in Unity, as a assignment for lecture "Graphics Programming"

Based on the [Sebastian's basic raytracing pipeline](https://github.com/SebLague/Ray-Tracing), we added **volumetric lighting (fog), reflection, refraction, textures (albedo, normal, roughness)** for more detailed and realistic view.

Co-worked with *[Seungmin Lee](https://veldic.github.io/)*
